problem: 45
title: "How Old Is the Rose-Red City?"
---

An English professor remarks that some poets write one immortal line and
nothing else worth remembering; John William Burgon's verse is forgotten, yet
he wrote "a rose-red city half as old as Time."

A mathematician friend replies with a verse in the same spirit: the city is
half as old as Time. One billion years ago, the city's age was exactly
two-fifths of what Time's age will be one billion years from now. How old is
the city today?
