title: "Crossing the Desert"
---

A desert is 800 miles wide. Unlimited gasoline is available at one edge, and none
exists anywhere in the desert. A truck can carry enough fuel to travel 500 miles
(call that one "load"). It may set up fuel caches anywhere along the route, of
any size, and fuel in a cache never evaporates.

What is the least total amount of gasoline, measured in loads, that the truck
needs to get across? Is there any upper limit on the width of desert it could
cross?
