title: "The Counterfeit Coins"
---

You have ten stacks of coins, each stack holding ten coins. Every genuine coin
weighs a known amount, but one entire stack is counterfeit: each of its coins
weighs a fixed, known amount more than a genuine coin (say one gram heavier). The
coins all look identical.

Using an accurate pointer scale that reads out an exact weight (not a two-pan
balance), and taking just **one** weighing, how can you determine which stack is
the counterfeit one?
