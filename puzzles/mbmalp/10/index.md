title: "The Touching Cigarettes"
---

Some small objects can be arranged so that every one of them touches all the
others: four identical golf balls can be grouped this way, and so can five
identical half-dollar coins.

[Figure: on the left, four golf balls clustered together, three forming a base
with the fourth resting on top, every ball in contact with the other three. On
the right, five coins arranged in a mutually touching group (the drawing shows
three of them tilted against one another and resting on a flat coin).]

Can you position **six** identical cigarettes so that each one touches each of
the other five? The cigarettes may not be bent or broken.
