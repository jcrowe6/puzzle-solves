problem: 14
title: "The Twelve Matches"
---

You have twelve matches, each one unit long. Laid end to end on a table and
joined only at their tips, they can enclose a polygon - for example a 3x3 square with an area of 9, or a "plus sign" shape with area of 5.

Using all twelve matches (each lying flat and full length, no breaking or
overlapping), arrange them into a closed figure that encloses an area of exactly
**4** square units.
