title: "The Amorous Bugs"
---

Four bugs, labeled A, B, C and D, sit at the corners of a square whose sides
are 10 inches long. (A and C are male; B and D are female.) At the same moment,
each bug sets off toward the next one around the square: A heads for B, B for C,
C for D, and D for A. They all move at the same constant speed, always aiming
directly at their target, so each traces a logarithmic spiral, and the four
identical spirals converge at the center of the square.

[Figure: a square with A at the top-left, B at the top-right, C at the
bottom-right and D at the bottom-left; each side is labeled 10. Dashed arrows
run along the sides in the direction A→B, B→C, C→D, D→A, showing each bug's
initial heading.]

How far does each bug travel before they all meet? The problem can be solved
without calculus.
