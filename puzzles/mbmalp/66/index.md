Scott Kim, who says he invented this puzzle in sixth grade, cut a large capital
letter out of paper and then gave it a single fold.

[Figure: a three-dimensional-looking shape resembling a capital L (or another
letter) folded once: a flat vertical bar on the left with a horizontal arm
extending to the right at the bottom, and a second folded flap on top angled
back, so the overall silhouette suggests a folded letter.]

It is easy to see the letter could be an L, but that is **not** the letter Kim
cut. Which letter is it?
