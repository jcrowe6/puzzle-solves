Seven symbols are shown in a row, looking something like an ancient script. Each
symbol has a meaning, and if you work out what the series is, you can draw the
eighth symbol in the empty square at the end.

[Figure: a row of seven symbols followed by an empty square. In order, they are
roughly: a block-like bracket shape (like a squared-off letter M or an upside-down
U with extra stroke), a heart-like loop, the numeral 8 shape, a letter M with a
crossbar-like flourish, a rounded figure with a short horizontal stroke on top, a
pair of linked loops, and a V-shaped symbol. The scan's rendering is loose, so
check the original for the precise forms.]
