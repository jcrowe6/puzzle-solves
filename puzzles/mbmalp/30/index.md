title: "Water and Wine"
---

In the well-known puzzle, one beaker holds water and another holds wine. Some
water is moved into the wine, then the same volume of the resulting mixture is
moved back. Is there now more water in the wine than wine in the water? (They
turn out to be equal.)

Raymond Smullyan asks a follow-up. Suppose one beaker starts with 10 ounces of
water and the other with 10 ounces of wine. You repeatedly shuttle 3 ounces
from one beaker to the other and back, stirring after each transfer, as many
times as you like. Can you ever reach a state in which both beakers have the
same percentage of wine?
