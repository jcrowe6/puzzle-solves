title: "Time the Toast"
---

Three slices of bread must be toasted on both sides and buttered, as quickly as
possible. The toaster is the old-fashioned kind with hinged side doors: it holds
two slices at once and toasts only one side of each slice at a time, so to do
the other side you open the doors and flip the slices.

Timings:
- Putting a slice into the toaster: 3 seconds.
- Taking a slice out: 3 seconds.
- Flipping a slice in place (without removing it): 3 seconds.
- Toasting one side: 30 seconds.
- Buttering one slice: 12 seconds.

Both hands are needed for each insert, removal or flip, so only one slice can be
inserted, removed or flipped at a time, and you can't butter a slice while
another is being inserted, removed or flipped. Each slice is buttered on one
side only, and only after that side has been toasted. A slice already toasted
and buttered on one side may go back in for toasting on its other side. The
toaster starts out warm.

What is the shortest total time to get all three slices toasted on both sides
and buttered?
