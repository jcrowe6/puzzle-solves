title: "Scrambled Box Tops"
---

Three boxes hold marbles: one contains two white marbles, one contains two black
marbles, and one contains one white and one black. Their lids are labeled
**WW**, **BB**, and **WB** accordingly — but every single label is wrong.

You may draw marbles out one at a time, without looking inside a box, and you
want to determine the true contents of all three boxes. What is the fewest draws
that guarantees you can correctly relabel every box, and how?
