title: "Bronx vs. Brooklyn"
---

A young man rides the subway to visit one of two girlfriends — one lives uptown
in the Bronx, the other in Brooklyn. He has no preference, so he simply takes
whichever train arrives first at the platform. Bronx trains and Brooklyn trains
each arrive on a regular 10-minute schedule, and he shows up at the platform at
random times.

Over many months he finds he visits Brooklyn about nine times as often as the
Bronx. How can the train schedule account for this lopsided result?
