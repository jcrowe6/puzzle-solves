title: "A Fixed-Point Theorem"
---

At sunrise one morning a monk starts up a tall mountain along a narrow path that
spirals around the mountain to a temple at the summit. He moves at varying
speeds, stopping several times to rest and to eat. He reaches the temple just
before sunset. After several days of fasting and meditation he heads back down
the same path, again setting out at sunrise, again walking at varying speeds
with many pauses. His average speed going down is higher than his average speed
going up.

Prove that there is some spot on the path where the monk is at exactly the same
time of day on both journeys.
