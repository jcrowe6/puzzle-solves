title: "The Sliding Pennies"

Six pennies start out in a triangular stack on a flat surface: one coin at the
top, two beneath it, three in the bottom row, with neighbors touching.

[Figure (start): six coins in a triangle, rows of 1, 2 and 3 coins, each coin
touching its neighbors.]

The goal is to rearrange them into a ring of six coins surrounding an empty
central gap.

[Figure (goal): six coins forming a closed ring (hexagonal outline) with a hole
in the middle: two coins side by side at the top, one at the far left, one at
the far right, and two side by side at the bottom, each touching its two
neighbors in the ring.]

A move slides one penny, without disturbing any other coin, to a new place
where it touches **two** other coins. Coins must stay flat on the surface.
What is the fewest number of moves that gets from the triangle to the ring?
