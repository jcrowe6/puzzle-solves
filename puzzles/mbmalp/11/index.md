title: "Two Ferryboats"
---

Two ferryboats set out at the same instant from opposite banks of a river, each
crossing straight to the far side. Each travels at its own constant speed, but the
two speeds differ. They first pass each other 720 feet from one shore. On reaching
the far bank, each boat pauses for 10 minutes, then starts back; on the return trip they
pass each other 400 feet from the other shore.

How wide is the river?
