title: "Tricky Track"
---

Washington, Lincoln and Roosevelt high schools hold a track meet. Each school
enters exactly one athlete per event. Susan, a Lincoln student, watched only the
shot-put, in which her boyfriend, Lincoln's champion, took first place.

Later she reports that Washington won the meet with 22 points, while Lincoln and
Roosevelt each finished with 9. Every event awarded the same three point values
to first, second and third place, with first worth more than second and second
worth more than third, all positive whole numbers. Susan doesn't know how many
events there were.

Her brother asks whether there was a high jump, and she says yes. He asks who
won it, and she doesn't know. Remarkably, the information above is enough to
determine which school won the high jump. Which one was it?
