title: "Beer Signs on the Highway"
---

Smith is driving at a steady speed along a highway, his wife beside him. He
notes that the roadside signs for Flatz beer appear to be evenly spaced and
wonders how far apart they are.

Mrs. Smith times one minute on her watch and counts the signs they pass in that
minute. Smith observes that ten times her count exactly equals the car's speed
in miles per hour.

Assume constant speed, equal spacing, and that her minute began and ended with
the car halfway between two signs. How far apart are the signs?
