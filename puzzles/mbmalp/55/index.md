title: "Out with the Onion"
---

Four paper matches are laid on a table to form a martini glass: two matches
stand parallel as the bowl's sides, one lies across their bottoms as the base of
the bowl, and the fourth stands below the middle as the stem. A match head placed
inside the bowl stands for the olive-like onion of a Gibson cocktail.

[Figure: left, the glass made of four matches (two vertical sides, a horizontal
base and a vertical stem hanging below) with the onion as a dot in the bowl. Right,
two small diagrams of failed attempts: one where the onion is still enclosed, and
one where three matches were moved.]

Move exactly **two** matches so that the glass is rebuilt with the onion, which
must not move, ending up **outside** it. The reformed glass may be rotated or
flipped upside down but must have exactly the same shape as before.
