title: "Find the Oddball"
---

Given two balls, one heavier than the other, a balance scale tells you which
is heavier in one weighing. With four balls, all the same weight except for one
heavier ball, two weighings find it.

Now suppose you have nine identical-looking balls, one of which is heavier than
the other eight. What is the fewest number of weighings on a balance scale that
is guaranteed to identify the heavy ball?
