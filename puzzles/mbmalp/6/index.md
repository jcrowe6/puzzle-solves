problem: 6
title: "Cutting the Cube"
---

A carpenter has a wooden 3×3×3 cube and wants to saw it into twenty-seven unit
cubes. Making straight cuts with a saw, this clearly takes six cuts (two in each
of the three directions).

He wonders whether he can do it in **fewer** than six cuts if, between cuts, he
is allowed to stack and rearrange the pieces however he likes. Can the number of
cuts be reduced? Prove your answer.
