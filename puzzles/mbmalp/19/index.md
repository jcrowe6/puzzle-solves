title: "The Flight around the World"
---

A fleet of airplanes is based on a small island. Each plane's fuel tank holds
just enough to fly halfway around the world. Fuel can be shifted from one
plane's tank to another at any moment in flight, in any amounts. The only fuel
supply is on the island. Assume that refueling, in the air or on the ground,
takes no time.

What is the minimum number of planes needed to guarantee that one plane can fly
all the way around the world along a great circle? All planes fly at the same
constant ground speed and burn fuel at the same constant rate, and every plane
must make it back safely to the island base.
