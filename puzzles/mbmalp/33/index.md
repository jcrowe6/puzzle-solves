title: 'How Long Is a "Lunar"?'
---

In H. G. Wells's novel *The First Men in the Moon*, imagine the insect-like
inhabitants measure distance in a unit called the "lunar", chosen so that the
moon's surface area in square lunars is numerically equal to its volume in
cubic lunars. The moon's diameter is 2,160 miles. How many miles long is one
lunar?
