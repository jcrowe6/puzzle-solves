title: "The Returning Explorer"
---

An explorer starts at some point on Earth, walks exactly one mile south, then
one mile east, then one mile north — and finds himself back at his starting
point. The North Pole is the obvious place where this works.

Treating the Earth as a perfect sphere, are there any **other** starting points
on its surface from which this round trip returns you to where you began?
