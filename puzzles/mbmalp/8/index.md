title: "The Early Commuter"
---

Every day a commuter arrives at his suburban station at 5:00 and his wife, who
leaves home so as to reach the station at exactly that time, drives him the rest
of the way home. One day he catches an earlier train and reaches the station at
4:00. Rather than wait, he starts walking home along the route his wife drives.
She leaves home at her usual time, meets him along the way, picks him up, and
turns around. They reach home 10 minutes earlier than they normally would.

Assuming the wife always drives at the same constant speed and left home at her
usual time, how long was the husband walking before she picked him up?
