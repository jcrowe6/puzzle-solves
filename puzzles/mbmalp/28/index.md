problem: 28
title: "Dividing the Cake"
---

Two people can share a cake fairly with the "one cuts, the other chooses" rule,
so each is sure of getting at least half by their own judgment.

Devise a general procedure for $n$ people to divide a cake into $n$ portions
so that every one of them is satisfied that they received at least $1/n$ of
the cake.
