title: "What Price Pets?"
---

A pet shop owner bought some hamsters and half as many *pairs* of parakeets. He
paid $2 per hamster and $1 per parakeet, and marked every animal up by 10% over
its cost.

When all but seven of the animals had been sold, the money he had taken in
exactly equaled what he originally paid for the whole stock. So his potential
profit equals the combined retail price of the seven animals left. What is that
amount?
