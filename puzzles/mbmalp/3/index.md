title: "The Mutilated Chessboard"
---

Take a standard 8×8 chessboard and remove the two squares at diagonally opposite
corners, leaving 62 squares. You have 31 dominoes, each exactly the size of two
adjacent board squares.

Can you cover the mutilated board completely with the 31 dominoes — every square
covered, no domino hanging off the edge or overlapping another? Prove your answer.
