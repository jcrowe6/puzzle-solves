title: "How Did Kant Set His Clock?"
---

The philosopher Immanuel Kant lived so regularly that, as the story goes, people
in Königsberg set their clocks by seeing him pass certain landmarks.

One evening Kant found that his clock had stopped, because his manservant had
the day off and hadn't wound it. He didn't reset the hands, since his watch was
at the repair shop and he had no other way to find the correct time. He walked
to his friend Schmidt's house, about a mile away, and glanced at the hall clock
as he went in.

He stayed several hours, then walked home by the same route at his usual slow,
steady pace, which hadn't varied in twenty years. He had no idea how long the
return walk took (Schmidt had just moved to the neighborhood, and Kant had never
timed this route). Even so, as soon as he got home he set his clock to the
correct time.

How could Kant know the correct time?
