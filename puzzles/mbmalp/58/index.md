title: "Interrupted Bridge"
---

You are dealing a bridge hand and are about halfway through when the phone
rings. You set down the undealt part of the deck and answer. When you come back,
nobody remembers where the last card went, and no one has touched the cards
already dealt.

Without counting the cards in any player's hand, and without counting how many
cards are left to deal, how can you finish the deal quickly and correctly, so
that every player ends up with exactly the cards they would have had if the deal
had not been interrupted?
