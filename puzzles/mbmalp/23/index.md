collection: mbmalp
problem: 23
title: "Handshakes and Networks"
---

At a convention of biophysicists, some attendees shook hands with others, any
number of times each (possibly not at all). Prove that the number of people who
shook hands an **odd** number of times is even.

Here is the same question as a drawing: place any number of dots on a page and
join pairs of dots with any number of lines (a dot may have as many lines as you
like, or none). Prove that the number of dots that have an odd number of lines
touching them is always even.
