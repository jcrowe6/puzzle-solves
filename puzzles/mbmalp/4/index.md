problem: 4
title: "The Fork in the Road"
---

A traveler comes to a fork where one road leads to safety and the other to
ruin, but he doesn't know which is which. Two local men stand there. One of them
always tells the truth and the other always lies, and the traveler has no way of
telling them apart.

He is allowed to point at one road and ask **one** of the men a **single**
yes-or-no question. What question can he ask that will reveal, from the answer
alone, which road is the safe one?
