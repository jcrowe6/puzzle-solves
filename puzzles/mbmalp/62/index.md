This looks much easier than it is. Make a single cut, or draw one line (it need
not be straight), that splits the figure below into two identical parts.

[Figure: a zigzag-edged polygon, wider than tall, made of a rectangle-like
body with sawtooth edges: along the top, three triangular peaks and valleys
(smaller ones on the left, one larger peak toward the right); along the bottom,
three or four smaller zigzags; and notched points sticking out of the far left
and far right sides.]
