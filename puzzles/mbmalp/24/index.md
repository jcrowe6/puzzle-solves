title: "The Triangular Duel"
---

Smith, Brown and Jones fight a three-way pistol duel. They draw lots for the
shooting order, then stand at the corners of an equilateral triangle. Each fires
one shot per turn, rotating in that fixed order, until only one man is left
standing. On his turn a shooter may aim at whichever opponent he likes.

Their accuracy is common knowledge: Smith never misses, Brown hits 80% of the
time, and Jones hits 50% of the time.

Assuming everyone plays the best strategy and nobody is hit by a stray shot not
meant for him, who has the best chance of surviving? As a harder follow-up, what
are the exact survival probabilities of all three men?
