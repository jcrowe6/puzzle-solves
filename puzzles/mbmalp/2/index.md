title: "Draw Poker"
---

Two players draw cards from a single ordinary 52-card deck to each build the best
possible five-card poker hand. The deck is face up, so each player can see and
deliberately choose which cards to take; they draw alternately, the first player
going first, until each holds five cards.

The first player claims that with correct play he can always guarantee a win.
Which five cards should he aim to collect, and why does that choice assure that
the second player can never beat him?
