title: "White, Black and Brown"
---

Professor Merle White (mathematics), Professor Leslie Black (philosophy) and
Jean Brown (a young stenographer in the admissions office) are having lunch.

The woman remarks that their surnames are Black, Brown and White, and that one
of the three has black hair, one brown and one white. The black-haired person
replies that none of them has hair matching their own surname. Professor White
agrees.

If the woman's hair is not brown, what color is Professor Black's hair?
