title: "Acute Dissection"
---

Can a triangle with one obtuse angle be cut into smaller triangles that are
all acute (all three angles under $90^\circ$; a right angle counts as neither
acute nor obtuse)? If it is impossible, prove it. If it can be done, what is the
smallest number of acute triangles into which any obtuse triangle can be cut?

[Figure: a long obtuse triangle with its obtuse angle at the left. Cuts split it
into four pieces labeled 1 to 4: a large piece 1 on the left, then 2, 3 and 4
across the bottom toward the right. Pieces 1 to 3 are acute but piece 4 is
obtuse, so this attempt gains nothing.]

Related question: what is the smallest number of acute triangles into which a
**square** can be dissected? The proposer found nine, then eight, suspects eight
is minimal, but could not prove it. Can you find eight, or fewer?
