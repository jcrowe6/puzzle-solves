You make jewelry and want to join four separate pieces of silver chain, each a
short run of three links, into one circular bracelet.

[Figure: left, four short chains of three links each; right, the finished

Cutting a link open and welding it shut again is tedious, so you want to cut as
few links as possible. What is the minimum number of links you must cut?
