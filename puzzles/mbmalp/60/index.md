title: "Move the Queen"

A chess queen starts on a square one step in from a corner of the board. The
task is to make **four** queen moves (any distance horizontally, vertically or
diagonally) so that her path passes through all nine shaded squares.

[Figure, left: the upper-left region of a chessboard with the queen on the
second square down in the leftmost column. Nine squares are shaded: a 3×3 block
occupying the three leftmost columns of the top three rows, with the queen on
the left-middle square of that block. Figure, right: a smaller sketch of the
nine-square shape drawn as a plus sign (a vertical bar and a horizontal bar of
three each, overlapping), with a six-move path traced over it as an example.]

A six-move route exists, but that is two moves too many.
