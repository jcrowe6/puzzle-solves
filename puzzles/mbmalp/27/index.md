title: "The Lonesome 8"
---

A puzzle that readers of a mathematics journal voted its most popular, first
published in 1954. A numerologist has been testing, by calculator, all of the
roughly $81 \times 10^9$ ways to fill in the missing digits of the long division
below. In it, every digit has been replaced by an x except for a single 8 in the
quotient.

```
          . . 8 . .          (quotient: only the 8 is shown)
      ______________
 xxx ) xxxxxxxxx
       xxx
       ----
        xxxx
         xxx
        -----
          xxxx
          xxxx
          -----
```

The division is exact (no remainder). The 8 is the third digit of a five-digit
quotient. Reconstruct the division; there is exactly one way to do it.
