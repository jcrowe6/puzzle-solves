They must pick all 4 "10" cards, then any other card for the 5th.

The best hand is a straight flush (SF). With all of the 10's, the first player is the only one who could ever complete an AKQJ10 SF, (an A-high SF) or a K-high, Q-high, J-high, or 10-high SF.

The highest hand the second player can make now is a 9-high SF. Their only hope of winning is to "block" the first player from making a 10-high SF or better (on the 1st player's 2nd draw), with their (the second player's) first draw.

To do this, they need to take 1 card from each suit of rank A-J AND 9-6. That is 8 cards at minimum.

With only 5 cards to take, the second player will always leave an available SF for the first player to complete on their second draw, of value 10-high or better.

With 9-high SF remaining the second player's best possible hand, the first player always wins with this strategy.
