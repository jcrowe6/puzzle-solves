I've heard this one before so I decided to answer in the form of a poem.

> "Which way would the other say?"
> Is the question for that day
>
> For if the liar you happen to ask
> Will be given an impossible task
>
> Lie, he will, with all his might
> And say "go left" if the truth says right
>
> But if you ask the one that's true
> He will tell of the liar to you
>
> And truthfully say that he would lie
> And lead you down the path to die
>
> So no matter which you ask the question
> Follow the opposite of their suggestion!
